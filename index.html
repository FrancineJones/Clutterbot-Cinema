Patterns we keep noticing
Direct observations from the reports.
Audience exit
Most leave fast, a few linger
At Event Queensgate, most of the audience cleared in ~4 minutes; the last people left around 12 minutes after the credits. Embassy was similar — full clearance at ~9 minutes.
Full house, light mess
High occupancy ≠ heavy clean
Event Queensgate was ~80% full and "some rows required little to no cleaning." United Mt Maunganui at 90–95% capacity was cleared with brush and shovel only.
Reusables first
Glasses are collected before rubbish
Staff at Embassy and Event ran a separate wine-glass pass before the main clean. Often a different staff member handles this sub-routine.
Popcorn vs vacuums
Vacuums clog, so staff use a brush
Roxy staff described popcorn as the most problematic waste because it spreads and clogs vacuums. Brush + shovel is their default tool.
Aisle staging
Rubbish piles up on aisle tables first
Event crews gather rubbish onto aisle seat tables, then bag it. Drinks are poured into empty popcorn cups before being moved.
Pocket fallout
Small items end up between seats
Embassy report notes "many things that appeared to fall out of pockets between seats." A different handling category from food waste — some items are lost-property.
